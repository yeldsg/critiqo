Indie Pop Bedroom Pop Soft - Rock


“Bags” is often cited as a queer anthem for its subtle yet emotionally potent expression of same-sex attraction. Clairo (Claire Cottrill) has spoken in interviews about the song’s autobiographical nature—describing the uncertainty and thrill of trying to understand a new, potentially romantic relationship with a female friend. Instrumentally, the song features minimalist percussion, ambient synths, and reverb- heavy guitar. The song’s structure avoids a traditional chorus, opting instead for repeated lines and cyclical melodies that mirror overthinking and emotional loopiness. “Bags” was nominated for Best Rock Song at the 2020 Libera Awards and remains a staple in Clairo’s setlist due to its raw resonance with fans.
