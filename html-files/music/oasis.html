Brit - Pop Rock


Despite being written in a haze, “Don’t Look Back in Anger” has taken on profound meaning over the decades. With its opening line directly referencing John Lennon ("So Sally can wait"), the track merges Beatles-inspired melody with stadium rock sensibility. It was sung by Liam Gallagher, though Noel Gallagher often performs it acoustically. The song became a spontaneous symbol of unity and defiance after the 2017 Manchester bombing. Crowds at memorials sang it, transforming its philosophical theme into a collective balm. Musically, it’s built around a piano riff similar to “Imagine,” and ends with a cathartic, chantable chorus that embodies its plea to release anger and bitterness.
