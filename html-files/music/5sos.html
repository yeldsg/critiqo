2015

Sounds Good Feels Good


Artists: 5SOS, Luke Hemmings, Michael Clifford, Calum Hood, and Ashton Irwin.


Producer: Luke Hemmings, Calum Hood, Michael Clifford, Ashton Irwin (all 5SOS members), and John Feldmann.


Label: Capitol Records, Hi or Hey Records.


Key Themes: Emotional scars, young love, rebellion, healing


Language: English


Pop punk Alternative Rock


"Safety Pin" presents a metaphor of emotional patchwork—using the image of a safety pin as a way to fasten broken parts back together. The lyrics are filled with a mix of self -deprecation and hope: the protagonists see themselves as flawed and misunderstood, but still believe love can “fix” them. It speaks to teenagers or young adults who feel cast aside, aligning with the band’s emo-punk influences like Green Day and Blink-182. The song balances upbeat instrumentation—sharp drums, layered guitars—with vulnerable lyrics, making it both moshable and emotionally charged. It reflects a time when 5SOS was transitioning from a teen pop band to artists exploring more serious themes of depression, therapy, and self-worth (as seen throughout Sounds Good Feels Good).
