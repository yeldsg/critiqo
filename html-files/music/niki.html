Head in the Clouds II


Artists: NIKI


Producer: NIKI and Jacob Ray


Composers & Songwriters: Nicole Zefanya (NIKI)


Label: 88rising, 12Tone Music


Key Themes: Distance, emotional detachment, career vs. love, longing


Language: English


Contemporary R&B Pop ballad


In “La La Lost You,” Indonesian-born singer-songwriter NIKI captures the quiet devastation of losing someone not because of betrayal, but because of life’s divergence. The reference to "La La" suggests both Los Angeles and a dreamlike disorientation—something beautiful but fading. NIKI’s writing style often incorporates storytelling with deeply specific imagery (e.g., "We were in the backseat / drunk on something stronger than the drinks in the bar"). Live performances of the song, especially on 88rising's Asia Rising Forever livestream, highlight her emotional control and vocal strength. The sparse instrumental arrangement gives space for her voice to convey heartbreak with sincerity. The song’s popularity exploded on platforms like TikTok and YouTube, establishing NIKI as a voice of Gen Z emotional depth.
