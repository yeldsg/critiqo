Reveal


Artists: The Boyz – South Korean boy group known for powerful vocals and synchronized choreography


Producer: Ollipop, Hayley Aitken, and Daniel Davidsen


Composers & Songwriters: Ollipop, Daniel Davidsen, Hayley Aitken, and Danke (Lalala Studio)


Label: Cre.ker Entertainment


Key Themes: Hidden desires, dual identity, vulnerability, longing


Language: Korean


K - Pop R & B EDM


"REVEAL" marks a significant milestone in THE BOYZ's discography as their first full studio album's title track. The concept of the “wolf boy” reflects both strength and emotional depth, contrasting primal desire with poetic sensitivity. Musically, it’s a genre blend of electronic dance elements with smooth R&B layers and a dramatic pre-chorus that builds into a powerful hook. Lyrically, it discusses the fear and anticipation of confessing hidden love. The group's synchronization-heavy choreography drew attention during live performances, especially at Mnet stages and the group's road to Kingdom participation. Jo Beom-jin's direction for the music video amplifies the supernatural and romantic duality, using moon imagery and mirrors as recurring motifs to represent truth and transformation.
