Yeon Shi Eun is a model student who ranks at the top of his high school. Physically, Yeon Shi Eun appears weak, but by using his wits and psychology, he fights against the violence that takes place inside and outside of his school.

Weak Hero Class 1 (Hanja: Yakanyeongung Class 1) is a South Korean web series based on Naver webtoon Weak Hero by writer Seopass and illustrated by Kim Jin-seok (Razen), which was published in 2018. It stars Park Ji-hoon, Choi Hyun-wook, and Hong Kyung, directed and written by Yoo Soo-min and Han Jun-hee as the creative director. It is a strong action coming-of-age drama about a weak-looking boy, who fights against numerous violence with his friends.
