Mad Men is a critically acclaimed television series that aired from 2007 to 2015. Set in 1960s New York, it delves into the lives of advertising executives at the fictional Sterling Cooper agency on Madison Avenue. The show is renowned for its meticulous period detail, complex characters, and exploration of themes such as identity, societal change, and the American Dream.
