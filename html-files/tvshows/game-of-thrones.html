Game of Thrones is an American fantasy drama television series that aired on HBO from 2011 to 2019. Adapted from George R.R. Martin's A Song of Ice and Fire novels, the show consists of eight seasons and 73 episodes. Set on the fictional continents of Westeros and Essos, it follows multiple noble families vying for control of the Iron Throne and the Seven Kingdoms, while supernatural threats loom beyond the Wall in the North.
