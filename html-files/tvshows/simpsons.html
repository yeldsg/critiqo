The Simpsons is a long-running animated television series that has become a cornerstone of American pop culture. Premiering in 1989, it follows the lives of the Simpson family - Homer, Marge, Bart, Lisa, and Maggie - as they navigate the quirky town of Springfield. Known for its satirical humor, the show has influenced countless other series and remains a staple of animated television.

Paul Lawrence

This satire is very biting and is very much in the vein of Jean-Paul Sartre in the way it describes Realpolitik in ordinary life and the history of its development. The state in which the story is set is never revealed in the story (the location of the story simply being referred to as Springfield), either in motion picture or in the television series, the closest this revelation ever comes being a parody of a 1996 Guinness advert in which the answer to a question on how a pint of the iconic beverage fits into a human hand is muffled by a passing train; in the sending up of the advert in question, the answer to a question on to which state a Springfield-bound train is heading is also drowned out by automotive noise. However, by evidentially piecing together several different episodes, one may conclude that "The Simpsons" is set in Nebraska.
