Sit-Com Physical Comedy


Rowan Atkinson's Mr. Bean exemplifies the power of physical comedy to convey humor without words. Through exaggerated movements, precise timing, and a distinctive character attitude, Atkinson has created a timeless comedic figure that continues to entertain audiences across the globe.
